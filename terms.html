Prism ("the app") is a personal, single-user desktop tool operated by the owner of the "Trạm Giải Trí" channel. It runs locally on the owner's computer and is used to edit short videos and send them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

1. Who may use the app

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own TikTok account.

2. TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API (upload to inbox). Videos are sent to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts; nothing is published without the account owner completing the post in the TikTok app. Use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service.

4. Disconnecting

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) or by deleting the locally stored token.
